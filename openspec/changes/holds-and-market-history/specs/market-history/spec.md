## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Commodity prices over time
The exchange SHALL chart a commodity's market price over the last hour, six hours or day of world time on each exchange the empire has met (up to six at once), with a crosshair that reads every exchange's price at a year, a legend, and labels at the lines' ends. Each commodity row SHALL show a small trend line and its change over the chosen range. Past prices SHALL be the exchanges' own (as every client and the server work them out), without the player's own trading pressure.

#### Scenario: Watching metals
- **WHEN** the player opens the exchange and picks Metals
- **THEN** a chart shows the metals price on each exchange they have met over the last hour, and hovering it reads each price at that year
