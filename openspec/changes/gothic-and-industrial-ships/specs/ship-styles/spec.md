## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Classic cathedral ships
The Classic style SHALL build ornate cathedral warships: an armoured prow, decks rising toward the stern, a gothic tower with spires, buttresses and gilded trim. Weapons SHALL sit as broadside batteries. It SHALL have at least four archetypes chosen by seed and size, one of them art deco or Roman.

#### Scenario: A Classic battleship
- **WHEN** a large design uses the Classic style
- **THEN** it has a long armoured prow and a spired tower at the stern, with guns along both flanks

### Requirement: Industrial ships
An Industrial style SHALL build blocky, greebled, worn commercial ships like those in Alien. Its four archetypes SHALL be:
- a dropship;
- a tug;
- a tug towing a refinery;
- a bulk carrier.

It SHALL have its own hull plating. Research (Heavy Haulage) SHALL unlock it, and an empire SHALL be able to choose it at founding.

#### Scenario: Unlocking Industrial
- **WHEN** an empire researches Heavy Haulage
- **THEN** it can build and mix in the Industrial style

### Requirement: Style accents
Each style SHALL provide an accent: its signature pieces, mixed into another style's hull.

#### Scenario: Mixing Industrial into Technological
- **WHEN** a Technological design mixes in Industrial
- **THEN** industrial pieces (pipes, greebles, tanks) are added to its hull
